Cookie Notice

Last updated 5 November 2026

01What cookies are

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help the site work properly, remember your choices, and in some cases help the owner understand how the site is being used. Most websites use them in some form.

02How we use them

Our website does not set any cookies. There are no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not use Google Analytics or any similar tool. Nothing on the site follows you around the web, and we never sell information about our visitors.

03Other services on our site

Our website loads its fonts from Google Fonts and its photographs from Unsplash, and messages sent through our enquiry form are delivered to us by Web3Forms. None of these services sets cookies through our site. Like any website you visit, they receive your device's IP address so that they can deliver content to you, and their use of it is governed by their own privacy policies.

04Your consent

Because our website sets no cookies, we do not need to ask for your consent, and you will not see a cookie banner. If that ever changes (for example, if we add visitor statistics), we will update this notice and ask for your consent before any non-essential cookies are placed on your device.

05Managing cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings at any time. Most browsers let you block cookies or remove those already stored. Please note that blocking strictly necessary cookies may stop parts of the website from working as intended. Guidance for managing cookies in common browsers is available at aboutcookies.org.

06More information

For more on how we handle your personal information generally, please see our Privacy Policy. If you have any questions about our use of cookies, you can reach us at contact@cleankeeps.co.uk.
